What the system does

Student Management

Complete student records, searchable by name, roll number or department across every year of study.

Faculty Management

Faculty profiles tied to departments with courses and teaching assignments.

Departments & Courses

Organize departments, HODs and course catalog with credits, semesters and faculty mapping.

Attendance

Mark course-wise attendance and track overall and per-course percentages with defaulters below 75%.

Examinations & Marks

Create exams, enter marks and get automatic grade calculation on each student's marksheet.

Fee Management

Generate fee records, record full or partial payments and monitor outstanding balances.

Reports

Export student lists, attendance defaulters, result summaries and fee collection with CSV download.

Role Based Access

Admins run everything, faculty mark and grade, and students view their own attendance, marks and fees.
